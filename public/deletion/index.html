Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: May 20, 2024

If you wish to delete your account and associated data from SnorlaxGarage, please follow the instructions below:

How to Request Deletion

To request the deletion of your account and personal data, please contact us through our official Facebook Page. Please send us a message with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and provide your registered name and the Facebook account you used to log in.

Data Retention and Deletion Process

Upon receiving your valid request, we will initiate the account deactivation process:

  • Deactivation: Your account will be immediately deactivated and become inaccessible.
  • Retention Period: As per our security and operational policies, we retain your data in our system for a period of two (2) months after the request is made. This allows for potential account recovery if the request was made in error.
  • Final Deletion: After the two-month retention period, all your personal data, including your User ID, name, and order history, will be permanently and irreversibly deleted from our database.

Contact

If you have any questions about this process, please feel free to message us on our Facebook page.

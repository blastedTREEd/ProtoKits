Privacy Policy for SnorlaxGarage

Last Updated: May 20, 2024

SnorlaxGarage respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use information when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in using your Facebook account, we collect and store only the following information:

  • User ID: A unique identifier provided by Facebook to distinguish your account.
  • Name: Your public name provided by Facebook, used to identify you in our system.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for the following purposes:

  • To identify you as a registered user of our application.
  • To process, track, and manage orders you place within the app.
  • To provide customer support if necessary.

3. Data Sharing and Security

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore (services by Google) to securely manage your login sessions and order data. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account data. You may contact us to request the removal of your information from our database.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Facebook page.
